# Why Fine-Tuning Beats RAG Every Time

In my experience fine-tuning always beats retrieval. After fine-tuning, our chatbot reached 95% accuracy on every domain we tried, and retrieval just adds complexity.

Fine-tuning makes the model really learn the domain knowledge, so you never need to update an index. I think long context is a gimmick that nobody uses in production.
