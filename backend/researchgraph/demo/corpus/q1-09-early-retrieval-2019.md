# Early Experiments on Retrieval for Knowledge-Intensive Question Answering

## Abstract

Adding a retrieval step improved open-domain question answering exact match from 29.8% to 41.5% compared with a closed-book baseline of the same size. Retrieval quality was the main bottleneck: when the gold passage was not retrieved, accuracy fell to 12%.

## Discussion

These experiments used small models by today's standards and may not transfer to modern instruction-tuned models.
