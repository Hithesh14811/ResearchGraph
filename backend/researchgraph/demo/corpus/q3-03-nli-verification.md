# Detecting Unsupported Claims with Entailment Verification

## Abstract

An entailment-based verifier detected 83% of unsupported claims in generated answers with an 11% false-positive rate. Verification added a median of 350 milliseconds of latency per answer.

## Analysis

The verifier struggled with claims that combined information from multiple passages, detecting only 64% of those. Splitting answers into atomic claims before verification improved detection on compound claims.
