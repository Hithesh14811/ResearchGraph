# Learning When Not to Answer: Calibrated Abstention in RAG

## Abstract

Allowing the model to abstain when retrieved evidence was insufficient reduced the hallucination rate by 40% relative to always answering, while reducing answer coverage by 8%. Users rated abstentions as more helpful than incorrect answers in 71% of pairwise comparisons.

## Discussion

Abstention thresholds must be tuned per application: aggressive thresholds frustrated users when the knowledge base did contain the answer.
