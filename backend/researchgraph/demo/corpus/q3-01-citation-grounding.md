# Citation-Grounded Generation Reduces Unsupported Claims

## Abstract

Requiring the generator to cite retrieved passages for every sentence, combined with post-hoc citation verification, reduced the rate of unsupported statements from 21% to 7% on a long-form question answering benchmark. Citation requirements alone, without verification, reduced unsupported statements only to 15%.

## Results

Verification removed or rewrote 9% of generated sentences. Most removed sentences were summary statements that combined several sources without citing any of them.

## Limitations

Human annotation covered 600 answers in one domain.
