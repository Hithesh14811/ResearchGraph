# Measuring RAG Faithfulness in Production

Automated faithfulness scores from an LLM judge correlated with human judgments at 0.71 (Spearman) across 800 answers. The judge underestimated hallucinations in answers longer than 300 words.

Teams that tracked faithfulness weekly caught retrieval regressions an average of 9 days earlier than teams relying on user reports.
